# Novelty check — 10 September 2026

**No earlier resolution located; novelty not established.**
The mathematical counterexample is verified, but the literature search
has unresolved gaps. Do not describe it as a confirmed new discovery.

The [original Kourovka Notebook](https://alglog.org/21tkt.pdf), problem
21.137, printed p. 181, attributes the question to L. Wilson and includes
the exponent-eight case explicitly.

Searches covered the problem number, Wilson with power-subgroup terminology,
“squares form a subgroup”, “power closed”, “powerful”, the wreath-product
description, and SmallGroup(128,928). No indexed result found explicitly
settled this problem or gave the same square-subgroup counterexample.

Relevant older literature:

- Howen Chuah, [On Groups whose Squares are Subgroups](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/ims/bull88/wef/Classroom/Chuah/Chuah-wef.pdf),
  Irish Mathematical Society Bulletin 88 (2021), 69–77.
  The retrieved article studies square-subgroup conditions and examples;
  no wreath-product or order-128 occurrence was found.
- H. S. Sun, On groups whose squares form a group, Bulletin of the
  Institute of Mathematics, Academia Sinica 9 (1980), 381–388.
- M. Haugh and D. MacHale, The subgroup generated by the squares,
  Proceedings of the Royal Irish Academy A 97 (1997), 123–129.

The latter two were identified through Chuah's references; their full
texts were not obtained. Their contents are a material gap in the
priority check. They are not assumed to contain, or not contain, our result.
Results about the subgroup GENERATED by squares must also be distinguished
from the stronger condition that the square SET is already closed.

[GAP's character-table documentation](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Thomas.Breuer/ctbllib/ctbltoc/data/A8.2N2.html)
identifies our group as a Sylow 2-subgroup of S8 and SmallGroup(128,928).
The group itself is classical; only the application to the question could
be new. No author has been contacted and no claim of external peer review
is made.
