# Joining the first and third DAG auxiliary banks

For even h, pair ground points consecutively. For a triple A, exchange its
member in the first pair that contains exactly one member of A. The resulting
pi is a fixed-point-free involution, and A intersects pi(A) in two points.
Their binary indicator lines are norm one and orthogonal.

The new DAG's entire auxiliary bank has initial active frame D0 and terminal
active frame D1; this includes the center and every side role. Identify the
first-stage bank with spectators (A,B) with the third-stage bank with spectators
(B,pi(A)), keeping the same local role index. This is a bijection. Stages do
not overlap, and each invocation restores arbitrary scratch.

The first-stage final frame is E=F1 tensor <t_A> tensor <t_B>. The third-stage
initial frame is H=<t_B tensor t_pi(A)>^perp tensor F3. E is contained in H.
A coordinate unit outside A union pi(A) in factor two, any unit in factor
one, and t_B in factor three give a norm-one vector in H intersect E^perp.
Thus the nested residual is nonalternating and has an orthonormal basis.
Replacing E->full and zero->H by E->H saves exactly m edge dimensions and
removes one physical role. Therefore it preserves Delta while reducing W.

With R side roles and h compressed centers per invocation, this yields
W=2v^3+2v^2(R+h), Delta=2v^2(v-3h^2), m=h^3 for the complex motif.
This is the whole-bank version of the community stage-reuse construction;
it does not use the individual-neighbor role matching of the older experiment.
