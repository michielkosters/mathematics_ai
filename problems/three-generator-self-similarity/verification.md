# Verification of Kourovka 21.42

This is a deductive verification using published theorems. No Python or Sage calculation is needed, and no machine-checked proof is claimed.

1. **Check the question.** [Berlatto–Gentil, Problem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16947v1) asks for a three-generated torsion-free nilpotent group of class three that is not self-similar. This matches Kourovka 21.42.
2. **Check the Lie algebra hypotheses.** For any such group, its rational Malcev Lie algebra has class three and is generated by the logarithms of its three group generators. The BCH formula proves the generation assertion; see the short proof in [README.md](README.md).
3. **Check the grading input.** [Dekimpe–Deré (2016), printed p. 365, PDF page 19](https://www.tmna.ncu.pl/static/published/2016/v47n1-18.pdf#page=19) explicitly records positive gradings for all three-generated three-step nilpotent Lie algebras, crediting Dekimpe–Igodt–Pouseele (2003). This statement is not restricted to dimension six, although it is used there in a discussion of that dimension bound.
4. **Check the implication.** A positive grading has no degree-zero component; extending scalars to the complex numbers preserves that fact. [Mathieu, Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.11291v1) applies to the original finitely generated torsion-free nilpotent group and gives a free self-similar action with dense orbits. Such an action is faithful, so it answers the question.

All groups in the question are covered. No extra lattice restriction, dimension bound, or computational cutoff is imposed.
