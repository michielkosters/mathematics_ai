# Prior-art check — 10 September 2026

**Assessment: apparently new; priority unconfirmed.** We found no earlier
resolution in the sources and indexed searches below. A search cannot
establish that no unpublished or unindexed solution exists.

## Primary sources

- [Kida, On semiabelian groups](https://doi.org/10.1515/jgth-2024-0010),
  published online in 2024, journal volume 2025: Conjecture 1.3 states the
  implication refuted here. The reported search excludes counterexamples
  of order at most 240. Example 5.5 already supplies the important
  order-96 semiabelian group containing SL(2,3); this ingredient is credited
  to Kida, not claimed as our discovery.
- [Kourovka Notebook, 21st edition (2026)](https://alglog.org/21tkt.pdf),
  printed page 171: problem 21.68 still presents the conjecture.
  Its listing does not exclude a more recent resolution.
- [Kida's earlier lecture notes](https://www.rs.tus.ac.jp/kida/beppu.pdf)
  also discuss the conjecture. They do not supply our counterexample.

## Search scope and exclusions

Indexed searches combined “semiabelian” and “semi-abelian” with “monomial”,
“nonmonomial”, “counterexample”, “Kida”, “21.68”, “2592”, and “7776”.
Additional searches targeted arXiv and GitHub. No prior resolution was found.
The author's website root did not load; we do not claim to have checked
every item on that site.

Search hits for order-192 counterexamples concern the **converse**
(monomial need not imply semiabelian), not the statement refuted here.
Listings of groups of order 2592 alone do not establish prior publication
of this conjecture's resolution.

This check is separate from verification of the proof. No author or editor
has been contacted, and the result has not undergone external peer review.
